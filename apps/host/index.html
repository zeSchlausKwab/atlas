Place an image

Position a PNG or JPEG · publish a standalone Atlas

Your image becomes its own selectable raster layer. Compose it with any vector map in the client. Attribution and placement are saved inside the file.

Click two opposite corners, or enter coordinates. The image is stretched to this rectangle.

Up to 32 megapixels and 64 MiB after conversion. JPEG orientation is applied in your browser before upload.

North is the top of the image. Placement uses a Web Mercator rectangle; this is manual alignment, not a surveyed georeference. GeoTIFF and rotated images need a later importer.

Choose an image to calculate its zoom range and tile count.
