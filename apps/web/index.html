    Walking profile & coverage

    Shortest distance on retained OSM ways. Basic foot access, barriers and foot one-way rules. No terrain, difficulty, live closures or turn restrictions. Routes stay in this extract; they may be incomplete near its edges. Time assumes 4.8 km/h. Experimental, not navigation guidance.

      TAKE A PIECE WITH YOU

      Draw your offline world.

      Choose a boundary. Keep the map, the streets, and the paths between them.

      Uses your selected map layers, street names and POIs from Data. Add routing or search when needed.

      Open a package to start drawing.

      What stays inside the boundary?

      Whole display tiles intersecting your shape are retained, so the map can show beyond its edges. Routing and street segments are cut at the boundary. Search keeps places with a representative point inside. A simple polygon is supported; holes are not yet supported.

      After this app is saved for offline use, reopen it at this address and choose your downloaded .atlas file. Browser export is limited to 256 MB per file.
