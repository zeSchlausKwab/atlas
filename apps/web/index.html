      Runs the same request on this device and the server. Cell-enabled packages fetch only the routing regions explored by the search.

      Travel profiles & coverage

      Estimated travel times, mode-specific access, barriers, one-way rules and common via-node turn restrictions. No traffic, elevation costs or public transport schedules. Unsupported conditional and complex turn restrictions conservatively exclude incoming ways. Coverage ends at the extract boundary. Experimental, not navigation guidance.

        TAKE A PIECE WITH YOU

        Draw your offline world.

        Open a package to start drawing.

        What stays inside the boundary?

        Whole display tiles intersecting your shape are retained, so the map can show beyond its edges. Routing and street segments are cut at the boundary. Search keeps places with a representative point inside. A simple polygon is supported; holes are not yet supported.

        Memory & reuse

        Retained / budget. Meshes count vertex buffers; labels and directories use estimates. A visible view may exceed the mesh or label budget. Root metadata, active work, WASM heaps and regional search indexes are additional memory.
